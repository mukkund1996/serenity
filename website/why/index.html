WHY SERENITY

Less switching. More focus.

I usually run six to eight Claude sessions: feature and bug-fix work, plus a few for quick prompts, product work and product analytics. They lived in plain Claude windows, tmux panes, iTerm windows and VS Code terminals.

At some point I realized a big portion of my engineering was spent on switching contexts.

I wanted one persistent session that kept track of all the others, and took away the overhead of telling my teammates on Slack where everything stood. That became serenity's master agent.

Now I have a lot more focus. I get to any session quickly, and I delegate both the tasks and the status updates through the master.

It's for developers, product engineers, and anyone who uses Claude Code regularly.

— Mukkund
